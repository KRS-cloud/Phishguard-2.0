Project Overview

About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is a defensive B.Tech Computer Science project designed to demonstrate practical phishing-risk analysis using Python, Flask, machine learning and security-based detection rules.

What it does

Visitors can analyze suspicious URLs, email content and QR-code images for warning indicators. The platform provides educational risk estimates, detected signals and practical recommendations.

Password Security and the AI Assistant can also be used without creating an account. Accounts are optional and provide additional features such as personal scan history and dashboard analytics.

Project Scope

PhishGuard is designed as a defensive decision-support and cybersecurity-awareness platform. Its results should be treated as guidance rather than a guarantee that submitted content is completely safe or malicious.
