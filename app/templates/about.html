About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is a defensive B.Tech Computer Science project developed by Pankaj Pawar to demonstrate phishing-risk analysis with Python, Flask, security rules and a small machine-learning model.

What it does

Registered users can inspect URLs, pasted email text and QR images for warning indicators. The application presents an educational risk estimate and explains the signals it found. It does not visit a submitted website or claim to replace a browser, antivirus product or professional security review.

Open implementation

The project source is publicly available for review on GitHub.
