Plain-language Notice

Privacy

Account information

PhishGuard stores the name and email address supplied during registration, a one-way password hash, sign-in activity and the user's saved scan-history summaries.

Analyzer submissions

The service processes content you intentionally submit. Do not paste passwords, one-time codes, financial information, private keys, confidential messages or other sensitive data. QR images are decoded for analysis and are not intended to be retained as uploaded files.

External services

Neon provides the hosted database, Brevo delivers account recovery email, and Google Gemini may process questions sent to the optional AI assistant. Analyzer results can be generated without entering credentials from another service.

Control

Scan history belongs to the signed-in account. This student deployment is for demonstration and evaluation, so users should avoid submitting confidential or production data.
