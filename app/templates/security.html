Security and safe use

Never submit credentials

PhishGuard never asks for a Google, banking, college, workplace or other third-party password. The sign-in form accepts only a password created specifically for your PhishGuard account.

Passive analysis

URL and QR analysis examines text and extracted indicators; it does not open the destination. Email analysis examines pasted text and does not access your mailbox.

Understand the result

Scores are educational estimates from rules and a proof-of-concept model. False positives and false negatives are possible. A low-risk result is not a guarantee of safety.

Responsible scope

Use the platform only with content you are authorized to analyze. It is designed for awareness, classroom evaluation and defensive learning—not impersonation, credential collection or unauthorized testing.
