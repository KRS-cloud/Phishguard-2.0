PhishGuard does not visit the website. Scan history stores only the URL origin (for example, https://example.com), never its path, query string, fragment or embedded credentials. A low-risk result is not a guarantee of safety.

What PhishGuard Checks

  • HTTPS connection
  • Domain and subdomain structure
  • Suspicious phishing words
  • URL shorteners
  • IP-address based links
  • Encoded and hidden characters
  • Unusual domain extensions
  • URL length and special characters
