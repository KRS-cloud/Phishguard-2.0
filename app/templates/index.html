Analysis Process

How PhishGuard analyzes submitted content

01

Validate

Validate submitted URLs, email content and uploaded QR images without visiting linked destinations.

02

Analyze

Run security rules and machine-learning checks on the submitted content.

03

Explain

Convert technical findings into a clear explanation. Optional assistant requests may use Google Gemini.

04

Recommend

Provide a risk score, warning indicators and recommended next actions.
